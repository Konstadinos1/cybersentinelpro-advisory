02How we work

How an engagement runs

  1. 1

    Understand

    A short discovery: interviews with the people who run your systems, and a review of what you already have.

  2. 2

    Assess

    We evaluate against recognized frameworks — NIST CSF 2.0, and privacy obligations where they apply — and rank findings in plain language.

  3. 3

    Fix and verify

    You get a roadmap you own. We help you execute it, then re-test to confirm what changed.

Every engagement ends in writing: findings, priorities and the work done — ready to show your board or your auditor.
